### 4.1 Permitted Use
Multifamily residential (apartments, condominiums) and accessory uses (leasing office,
fitness/amenity space, structured or surface parking) are permitted by right in the R-4
district.

### 4.2 Bulk and Dimensional Standards
- Maximum building height: **65 feet or 5 stories**, whichever is less.
- Maximum Floor Area Ratio (FAR): **2.5**, computed as total gross floor area / lot area.
- Maximum lot coverage (building footprint as % of lot area): **60%**.
- Minimum front setback (from street right-of-way): **15 feet**.
- Minimum side setback: **10 feet** per side.
- Minimum rear setback: **20 feet**.
- Minimum landscape buffer along street frontage: **10 feet**, planted.

### 4.3 Parking Requirements
- Residential parking ratio: **1.25 off-street stalls per dwelling unit**.
- Up to **20% of required stalls** may be compact-car stalls (8.5 ft x 16 ft minimum).
- Accessible stalls: **1 accessible stall per 25 required stalls or fraction thereof**,
  minimum 1. At least one in every six accessible stalls must be van-accessible
  (12 ft stall + 8 ft access aisle, or 11 ft + 5 ft aisle per state accessibility code).
- Two-way drive aisles serving 90-degree parking: minimum 24 feet clear width.
- Bicycle parking: 1 space per 4 units, minimum 10.

### 4.4 Fire and Life-Safety Access
- A fire apparatus access lane of at least **20 feet clear width** must be provided
  within 150 feet of every exterior point of a building's ground-floor exterior wall,
  measured as an approved vehicle travel route (per adopted Fire Code Appendix D).
- Dead-end fire apparatus access lanes longer than 150 feet require an approved
  turnaround.
- Maximum common-path-of-travel / dead-end corridor length inside a building: **50 feet**.
- Maximum travel distance from any occupied space to the nearest exit stair enclosure:
  200 feet (sprinklered occupancy).

### 4.5 Accessibility (ADA / State Accessibility Code)
- An accessible route, minimum **44 inches (4 ft) clear width**, running slope
  5% max (cross slope 2% max), must connect accessible parking, the public
  right-of-way, and the accessible building entrance(s).
- A minimum of **2% of dwelling units** (or 1, whichever is greater, per building)
  must be fully accessible mobility units; an additional 2% must be communication-
  feature accessible units.

### 4.6 Stormwater and Landscape (Simplified)
- On-site stormwater management must retain the first **1 inch** of rainfall from all
  new impervious surface (simplified performance standard for this exercise; does not
  represent an engineered stormwater analysis).
- Surface parking lots over 20 stalls require interior landscape islands at a rate of
  1 island (minimum 9 ft x 18 ft) per 10 stalls.

### 4.7 Review Standard for This Exercise
Automated outputs referencing this article are schematic-level estimates only. Final
zoning compliance must be confirmed by a licensed professional and the Authority
Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) before permitting. Where information is insufficient to
evaluate a standard, the workflow should report the standard as **"unconfirmed"**
rather than assert compliance.
