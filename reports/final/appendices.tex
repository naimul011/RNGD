\section*{Appendix F --- Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Envelope from the app sidebar (default synthetic lot 170 x 265 ft). Street assumed on the south side.
\item Double-loaded corridor, 6 ft clear; room depth 26 ft; room width = program area / 26 ft.
\item Floor-to-floor: Level 1 16 ft, guest floors 10.75 ft (from the L+L elevations).
\item Core at the west end (Stair 1, two elevators, shafts); Stair 2 at the east end of the north side.
\item Rows whose count equals the number of guest floors (Ice, IT, Jan, Supplies) are placed once per floor.
\item Room mix spread evenly across floors, largest units first. Fixtures and furniture are typical, not from RNGD unit drawings.
\item Occupant loads use the RNGD CSV factors (IBC Table 1004.5); lobby as assembly unconcentrated (15 SF net).
\item 3D views are drawn with a painter's-algorithm axonometric in matplotlib. OpenGL renderers (moderngl, pyvista) are not installed in this environment.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Appendix G --- Open questions for RNGD}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Which area of the Level 1 / Level 2 drawing was meant? The reference coordinate note (scale 1.08 to the original image) did not name a room, stair or gridline. Please mark the spot on the L+L plans sheet.
\item Story limit: the program needs Level 1 plus five guest floors (six stories), but RNGD rule IBC-R1-007 allows five stories for sprinklered Type IIA R-1. Is a Type IA podium (IBC 510.2) intended, as in the L+L prototype?
\item Confirm the gross-up rule: kickoff meeting said 33\% circulation + 8\% wall burden; the L+L source sheets imply 1.25.
\item Real lot, setbacks, street frontage and fire-lane requirements (the envelope used here is synthetic).
\item Is parking part of this phase? It is not modelled.
\item Confirm the per-floor support rooms (Ice, IT, Jan, Supplies) and the Level 1 BOH arrangement.
\item Column grid: 30-34 ft bays assumed from the kickoff meeting. Is there a preferred structural module?
\item Prefab panel / module catalog to replace the indicative panel breakdown on the elevations.
\end{enumerate}
